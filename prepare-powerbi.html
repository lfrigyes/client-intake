Your intro call

A short video or phone call to confirm that we can help, agree the scope and package, and answer your questions. No files are needed before the call.

Agenda (20 minutes)

  1. Your goal and the decision behind it
  2. Scope, package and timeline
  3. What data exists and where it lives
  4. Confidentiality, price and next steps

Please have ready

  • The decision you need to make and by when
  • Who decides, and who gives us information
  • The name of your accounting software or where the figures are kept
  • Whether you want us to sign your confidentiality agreement, or use ours
  • Your rough budget, if you have one
Questions we will ask you on the call
  • Describe the decision in one sentence
  • Top 3 questions it must answer
  • Who reads it (roles)?
  • Client branding (logo, colours, font)? (+$100)
  • Logo file, HEX colours, font name
  • KPIs to track
  • Cost centres / business units
  • Is there a budget to compare against?
Documents we will ask for after the call
  • General ledger / trial balance export, 24 months (Excel/CSV)
  • Chart of accounts
  • Accounting software name (export format)
  • Budget file

After the call

  1. We send a confidentiality agreement and a written quote.
  2. You accept the quote and sign. Nothing is charged before that.
  3. We send a list of documents and a safe way to share them. Please do not email sensitive files.
  4. Work starts when the quote is accepted and the documents have arrived.

What we will not discuss

We do not give investment advice, ratings, price targets, trading or hedging suggestions, or certified valuations. If your goal falls under any of these, we will say so on the call.

Please do not send email addresses, phone numbers, passwords or bank details through this form. We contact you through the channel you used to reach us.
